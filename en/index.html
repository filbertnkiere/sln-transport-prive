      FAQ

      Good to know

      How do I book a ride?

      Get your price at the top of the page, then book in one click on WhatsApp or through the form. You can also call, text or email. Every booking is confirmed personally.

      Is the online estimate the final price?

      The estimate is based on the actual road distance. The final price is confirmed before your ride and does not change afterwards.

      How do I pay?

      By card, directly on board at the end of the ride.

      Do you work with businesses?

      Yes, it is a large part of what we do: business meetings, client pick-ups, seminars. An invoice is provided for every ride.

      Do you do long-distance trips?

      Yes, anywhere in France. Trips over 200 km receive a personalised quote.
